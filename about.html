📜 A Message to Parents

The Critical Window: Why Preschool Matters at Age 2

By Dr. (Mrs.) B. Arora • Founder Director, SHEMROCK Group of Schools

When your child is 2 years old, one of the most important decisions that you need to make is the choice of the right preschool for him or her. As 50% of the human brain development takes place from 2 to 4 years of age, it is absolutely critical that you make the right choice so that your child receives the right foundation for life.

“50% of brain development occurs between 2 to 4 years of age. The right preschool is not a luxury — it is the irrechargeable foundation for a lifetime of confidence, joy, and success.”

However, in the presence of a large number of preschools with varied facilities, making the right choice often becomes confusing. As loving parents, a number of vital questions might be running through your mind:

❓ Which is the right school for my child?
🏰 What facilities and safety are available?
📈 What are the previous learning results?
🎓 Who is the management — are they real educationists?
📜 What are their educational qualifications?
💖 Above all — will my child be happy?

After exploring SHEMROCK, you will get a clear glimpse of the rich, multifaceted life which we offer. You will realise that a child who enters into the cheerful and colourful corridors of SHEMROCK is instantly motivated to learn and explore everything that his or her curious young mind wishes to — in a loving, caring, encouraging, motivating, and deeply positive learning environment.

When your child completes his or her journey of learning at SHEMROCK, they will be fully prepared and confident to enter into the bigger setup of a formal school without anxiety or fear.

Your child deserves the very best, so I leave this privilege to you to decide for your child. However, the moment your child joins SHEMROCK, I would be happy to take him or her on a journey which is enjoyable, enriching, and rewarding.

I look forward to welcoming you and your little one to the SHEMROCK Family!

Dr. (Mrs.) B. Arora
