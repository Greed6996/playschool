A Legacy of Love, Nurturing & Academic Excellence

❓ Have Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to commonly asked questions from parents about our admissions, age criteria, curriculum, and safety.

Established in 1989, SHEMROCK is India’s first organized playschool chain. Over 35 years, we have nurtured over 4,50,000 children across 650+ branches in India, Nepal, and Bangladesh. Our proprietary curriculum integrates experiential learning, cognitive stimulation, and a home-like caring atmosphere.
Children can enroll starting from 1.5 years of age in our ShemFoundation (Playgroup) program. We offer 4 structured stages: Playgroup (1.5 - 2.5 yrs), Pre-Nursery (2.5 - 3.5 yrs), Nursery (3.5 - 4.5 yrs), and Kindergarten/Prep (4.5 - 5.5 yrs).
Our ShemPrep curriculum focuses on phonetic decoding, logical numerical reasoning, interactive conversational English, and social-emotional confidence. Our alumni consistently secure admissions in India’s leading formal day and boarding schools with flying colors.
All SHEMROCK branches adhere to strict child-safety guidelines: 24/7 CCTV surveillance, child-safe rounded furniture, anti-skid flooring, medical-grade daily sanitization, verified female support staff, and secure gated entry.
Core playschool hours generally run from 9:00 AM to 12:30 PM (varying slightly by branch). Many flagship centers also provide extended day care and after-school activity clubs till 6:00 PM for working parents.
